## 1) The supply number everyone quotes is not the supply you can buy

XCP's live supply is about **2.59M**, net of fee burns. But the market behaves like a **low-float asset** because a large portion is either:

- **Trapped** (defunct exchanges)
- **Lost** (never moved since the 2014 burn)
- **Sleeping** (multi-year dormancy)
- **Locked** (protocol escrow)

### Effective supply distribution

| Category | XCP | % | Status |
|----------|----:|--:|--------|
| General holders | 1,747,088 | 67.5% | Circulating (but mostly dormant) |
| Trapped on defunct exchanges | 421,593 | 16.3% | **Effectively lost** |
| Never-moved post-burn | 207,486 | 8.0% | Likely lost |
| Active CEX (Zaif + Dex-Trade) | 172,566 | 6.7% | Circulating |
| Protocol escrow | 41,533 | 1.6% | Locked |

**Key claim:** ~24% of supply (~629k XCP) is effectively not coming back to market under normal conditions. The "real circulating supply" is closer to **~1.92M XCP**, before you even account for dormancy.

### Where the trapped coins sit

|----------|------------:|---------------|--------|

These aren't "dormant"—they're **stuck**. Without legal action or bankruptcy proceedings, they're not coming back.

The never-moved burn addresses (207,486 XCP) tell a similar story: early adopters who burned BTC in 2014, received XCP, and never transacted. Not once. In 11 years. Almost certainly lost keys.

Even after removing lost/trapped/locked, the remaining supply is not actively circulating. It's sitting.

Half the supply hasn't moved in five years. That's not liquidity. That's a museum collection.

**Key claim:** XCP trades like an asset with a small active float sitting on top of a large dormant base. That combination is exactly how you get violent repricings: a thin marginal market with a big narrative surface area.

## 4) The whale alarm: Supply-Years Destroyed (SYD)

Most people use velocity as a proxy for activity. But velocity can be fake—same coins churning.

A better structural signal is **SYD (Supply-Years Destroyed)**: a proxy for whether *old supply is waking up*.

**The formula:** `SYD = LTDD ÷ (Supply × 365)`

Where LTDD sums `amount × days_since_last_activity` for each transaction. A SYD of 1.0 means the equivalent of the entire supply sitting idle for a year, then moving.

### How to read SYD

| Pattern | What It Means |
|---------|---------------|
| High velocity + low SYD | Churn/speculation (same coins moving) |
| High velocity + high SYD | Genuine distribution (old coins moving) |
| Low velocity + low SYD | Quiet accumulation (little disturbance) |

### The historical record

| Year | Price Δ | Velocity | SYD | What Happened |
|------|--------:|---------:|----:|---------------|
| 2014 | -85% | 2.57× | 0.36 | Post-burn capitulation |
| 2015 | +172% | 1.25× | 0.16 | Quiet rally |
| 2016 | **+1584%** | 2.72× | **0.81** | Distribution into strength |
| 2017 | -94% | 3.83× | **0.60** | Distribution into weakness |
| 2018 | -35% | 2.16× | 0.21 | Consolidation |
| 2019 | -28% | 0.97× | 0.04 | Quiet |
| 2020 | **+647%** | 0.25× | 0.05 | Organic rally |
| 2021 | -77% | 2.46× | 0.14 | Churn (same coins) |
| 2022 | +108% | 1.02× | 0.09 | Recovery |
| 2023 | +74% | 0.63× | 0.04 | Accumulation |
| 2024 | -76% | 0.39× | 0.06 | Capitulation |
| 2025 | +33% | 0.33× | 0.06 | Consolidation (YTD) |

### Reading the patterns

**2016 (SYD = 0.81, +1584%):** Early holders distributed into strong demand. The ICO boom absorbed the supply. Price 16x'd.

**2017 (SYD = 0.60, -94%):** Distribution continued, but demand dried up. Supply overwhelmed bids.

**2021 (Velocity = 2.46×, SYD = 0.14, -77%):** The "fake" activity. High velocity looks hot, but SYD was low. Same coins trading rapidly during NFT hype. No structural change.

**2020 (Velocity = 0.25×, SYD = 0.05, +647%):** The healthiest pattern. Quiet accumulation without disturbing dormant supply. Then price discovery.

**Key claim:** SYD doesn't predict direction. It predicts structural change. High SYD means old supply is moving—whether into strength or weakness depends on demand.

## 5) 2025 looks like 2019–2020

Here's the comparison that matters:

| Metric | 2019 | 2020 | 2025 YTD |
|--------|-----:|-----:|---------:|
| Velocity | 0.97× | 0.25× | 0.33× |
| SYD | 0.04 | 0.05 | 0.06 |
| Price Δ | -28% | +647% | +33% |

Same vibe: low churn, low old-supply disturbance, muted attention.

I'm not claiming "therefore +647%." Markets don't owe you symmetry.

I am claiming the on-chain structure is consistent with a low-float base. If demand returns while dormant supply stays dormant, there's not much float to absorb it.

## 6) What would break this thesis

If you want to be intellectually honest about "low float," you need to name the failure modes.

**1. Dormant supply wakes up**

A true supply crunch ends the moment old holders decide "this is my exit." Watch the SYD.

**2. Trapped balances re-enter circulation**

Unlikely without legal action, but possible. Poloniex showed activity as recently as 2024.

**3. New exchange listings expand the accessible float**

Currently only [Zaif](https://www.fsa.go.jp/en/regulated/licensed/en_kasoutuka.pdf) (Japan, FSA-approved) and Dex-Trade offer XCP. More venues = more liquidity = less convexity.

**4. Protocol activity stays low forever**

Low volume can mean exhaustion, or it can mean irrelevance. The difference is whether Counterparty activity (issuance, DEX usage, dispensers) returns.

## Appendix: Methodology

**Data source:** Full Counterparty event history (19.9M events), blocks 278,000–933,605 (Jan 2014–Jan 2025).

**Reconciliation:** Supply reconstructed from ledger entries matches protocol supply within **2 XCP**.

**Volume definitions:**
- *Transfer Volume:* XCP debits for `send`, `mpma send`, `sweep`
- *Settlement Volume:* XCP credits for `order match`, `dispense`, `btcpay`
- *Economic Volume:* Transfer + Settlement (used for velocity)

**SYD calculation:** Uses address-level "last-touch" proxy (LTDD). Not perfect coin-age (which would require FIFO/LIFO tracking). May overstate age for addresses with mixed-age holdings.

**Exchange tagging:** Conservative estimates based on known/identified wallets. "Trapped" figures are lower bounds.
